Privacy Policy

TierYourLife · last updated 2 September 2026

TierYourLife is an app for ranking things into tiers. This page says what it collects, why, and how to make it stop. It is written to be read rather than to be defensible, so where something is unclear the answer is the narrower one.

You can use it without an account

Making boards, ranking them and keeping them on your phone needs no account and sends us nothing. That data never leaves the device.

Signing in with Google, publishing a board, following an author or ranking somebody else's list are the parts that involve a server.

What is collected

WhatWhenWhy
Your Google account's email address, name and profile picture When you sign in To know whose boards are whose, and to show an author beside a published list
Your boards — titles, tiers, cards, and the pictures on them Only while "Back up my boards" is on So they survive a lost or changed phone
Lists you publish, and copies of the pictures on them When you publish Publishing means other people can see them
Who you follow, and lists you have taken to rank yourself When you follow or take one To build your feed and to order lists by how many people ranked them
Complaints you file about a list When you report one So somebody can read them and act
Crash reports and diagnostics If the app crashes To find out what broke

Search terms you type when adding cards are sent to the catalogues that answer them — see below. Nothing you type into a board title, a card name or a note is sent anywhere unless you publish that board or have the backup on.

Who else sees it

WhoWhat reaches them
Google Firebase — sign-in, database, file storage, functions, crash reporting Everything above. Firebase runs the servers this app talks to.
TMDB What you type when searching for a film, and the language you use
Wikidata What you type when searching, and the language you use

Nothing is sold, and nothing is handed to advertisers. There is no advertising in this app and no analytics beyond crash reports.

Ending your account

In the app: Settings → your account → Delete account. That removes the account, the copy of your boards, everything you published and the pictures behind it. It cannot be undone. Boards on your phone stay on your phone, and you can remove the app to remove those.

If you cannot reach the app, write to the address below from the email address the account uses, and it will be done by hand.

How long things are kept

Until you remove them, or until you end the account. Two exceptions: a board you delete waits in the trash for 30 days so you can change your mind, and an account signed in as a guest — one that was never given an email address — is swept away after 30 days of not being used.

Children

This app is not aimed at children under 13 and is not intended for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
