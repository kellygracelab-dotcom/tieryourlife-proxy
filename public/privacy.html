Privacy Policy

TierYourLife · last updated 20 November 2026

TierYourLife is an app for ranking things into tiers. This page says what it collects, why, and how to make it stop. It is written to be read rather than to be defensible, so where something is unclear the answer is the narrower one.

You can use it without an account

Making boards, ranking them and keeping them on your phone needs no account and sends us nothing. That data never leaves the device.

Signing in with Google, publishing a board, following an author or ranking somebody else's list are the parts that involve a server.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is handed to advertisers. There is no advertising in this app. Apart from crash reports, the only statistics are the anonymous daily totals described under Usage statistics, counted on our own server with no analytics company involved.

Usage statistics

We count how the app and the website are used, on our own server. What is stored is daily totals: a number for each kind of event on each day, split by platform (Android or web), and, when the app sends them, by app version and by the language and region set on your device or in your browser. The region is never worked out from your IP address. Your account, your device's ID, an advertising ID and your IP address are not stored with these totals.

The server counts the requests it answers anyway:

  • lists, topics and sections opened;
  • lists taken to rank, published and followed;
  • searches, and whether they found anything;
  • accounts deleted;
  • its own errors and refusals.

Once a night it also counts accounts: how many guests and Google accounts there are, how many are new, and how many were used. Only the numbers are kept.

To count each person once a day, the server keeps a one-way key made from the day, the platform and your account. It cannot be turned back into your account, is used for nothing else, and is deleted the next night.

The app also sends its own statistics, listed in the table above, at most once per visit. Your device remembers the day you first opened the app and the last day it sent anything, and sends only how many days have passed, so two visits cannot be joined into one person.

To stop it, turn off Settings → Send usage statistics in the app. The app then sends none of its own statistics. The requests it makes anyway, such as opening a list or searching, are still counted in the daily totals above, because counting them is part of answering them, and they never carry who made them.

Searches are counted by what was typed: lower-cased, with spaces tidied, and cut to 60 characters. A search that looks like an e-mail address or a phone number, or has six or more digits in a row, is not counted at all. Search counts are deleted after 90 days. A search counted fewer than three times in the period being looked at is never shown.

Only the app's owner sees these statistics. They are not sold or shared with anyone.

Ending your account

On the website, signed in: Settings, then Delete account at the bottom of the page. In the app: Settings → your account → Delete account. Either removes the account, the copy of your boards, everything you published and the pictures behind it. It cannot be undone. Boards on your phone stay on your phone, and you can remove the app to remove those.

In a web browser, the account's boards go from the browser it was deleted in. Boards made there without signing in stay: delete them under Your lists and empty the Trash, or clear the site's data in the browser's settings. The account's boards in any other browser it was used in stay there too, hidden; clearing the site's data in that browser removes them.

If you can reach neither, write to the address below from the email address the account uses, and it will be done by hand.

How long things are kept

Until you remove them, or until you end the account. Two exceptions: a board you delete waits in the trash for 30 days so you can change your mind, and an account signed in as a guest — one that was never given an email address — is swept away after 30 days of not being used. Usage statistics are daily totals with nobody in them and are kept; the search counts in them for 90 days, and the one-day key until the next night.
