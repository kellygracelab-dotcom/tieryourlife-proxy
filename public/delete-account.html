Deleting your TierYourLife account

On the website

  1. Sign in with the account.
  2. Open your account menu at the top right, and choose Settings.
  3. At the bottom of the page, press Delete account, and confirm.

In the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your account at the top.
  3. Tap Delete account, and confirm.

By email

If you can reach neither, write to kellygracelab@gmail.com from the email address the account uses. Say that you want the account deleted. It will be done by hand.

What goes

  • The account itself, and the ability to sign in with it
  • The copy of your boards kept for it
  • Every list you published, and the pictures on them
  • Who you follow, and who follows you
  • Any pictures of your own held for your account

What stays

  • Boards on your phone. They were made there and they stay there — remove the app to remove them.
  • Boards in a web browser. Deleted on the website, the account's boards go from that browser with it, but boards made there without signing in stay: they show under Your lists whenever nobody is signed in. Delete them there and empty the Trash, or clear the site's data in the browser's settings, which removes everything the site kept in that browser.
  • The account's boards in any other browser it was used in. They stay there, hidden, since nobody can sign in to show them; clearing the site's data in that browser removes them.
  • The number of people who ranked somebody else's list. Your name comes off it; the total belongs to their list.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. There is no waiting period and no way to recover the account afterwards.

Deleting some of your data, keeping the account

You can remove parts of what is kept without ending the account.

A list of your own

  • On the website: in Your lists, open the list's ⋮ menu (or right-click it) and choose Delete; inside a list, ⋮ More about this list → Delete list.
  • In the app: on Your lists, long-press the list and tap the trash icon.
  • A deleted list goes to the Trash (on the website, the Trash button on Your lists; in both, Settings → Your data → Trash), where you can restore it. It stays there until you remove it: press Remove on it, or ⋮ Empty trash, to delete it for good.
  • A list you published is taken down from the site first, then deleted.

A list you published

  • On the website: open the list, press Published, then Take it down; or, on its public page, ⋮ More about this list → Take it down.
  • In the app: open the list and tap ⋮ for its List settings, then Take it down under Publication.
  • It leaves the site and its link stops working; you keep the list itself.

The copy of your boards in your account

  • On the website: Settings → Your data, turn off Back up my lists and choose Stop and delete.
  • In the app: Settings, turn off Back up my lists under your account and choose Stop and delete.
  • The account's copy is deleted; the lists on the device you did it on stay there.

Usage statistics

  • In the app: Settings → Your data, turn off Send usage statistics. The app then sends none of its own statistics, and what was waiting to be sent is dropped.
