Deleting your TierYourLife account

In the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your account at the top.
  3. Tap Delete account, and confirm.

By email

If you no longer have the app, write to kellygracelab@gmail.com from the email address the account uses. Say that you want the account deleted. It will be done by hand.

What goes

  • The account itself, and the ability to sign in with it
  • The copy of your boards kept for it
  • Every list you published, and the pictures on them
  • Who you follow, and who follows you
  • Any pictures of your own held for your account

What stays

  • Boards on your phone. They were made there and they stay there — remove the app to remove them.
  • The number of people who ranked somebody else's list. Your name comes off it; the total belongs to their list.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. There is no waiting period and no way to recover the account afterwards.
